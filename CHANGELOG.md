# What's new on soulnicolas.com

Short version numbers: 5.0, 5.1, 5.2 ... A small change goes up by one step (5.3 to 5.4); a big redesign starts a new first number (6.0). The numbering was set to 5.0 on Nov 18, 2026 to match the work already done.
The newest version is on top, and it is the number shown to the owner at the bottom of Home and in Settings (visitors do not see it). Write each entry in plain words.
To release: add a new `## x.y · date` block at the top of this file in the same pull request. The build picks it up by itself.

## 5.8 · Nov 22, 2026
The privacy policy is brought up to date.
- It now says the newsletter is sent through Resend (not Google), that new sign-ups are kept on Cloudflare only, and that your reaction clicks in the newsletter are saved on Cloudflare.

## 5.7 · Nov 21, 2026
A proper Android icon.
- When Soul Vault is added to an Android Home Screen, the icon now fits the phone's round or square icon shape instead of being cropped. Nothing else changed.

## 5.6 · Nov 21, 2026
Your NuRenaissance account does more here.
- **Save songs**: when you are signed in, Now Playing has a ♡ Save button. Saved songs show on the Music tab as **Saved songs** and follow you to any phone or computer where you sign in.
- **Join the Soul Tribe in one tap**: right after you sign in, you are asked once if you want to join, with your account e-mail and name filled in (you still tick the box to agree to e-mails).

## 5.5 · Nov 21, 2026
Notifications live with your account; no Refresh link.
- The **Notifications** link at the bottom of Home now only shows once you are signed in with your NuRenaissance account, right next to it (the same in every NuRenaissance app). Phones that already turned them on keep getting them.
- The **Refresh** link at the bottom of Home is gone: the "A new version is ready. Tap to update" bar does that job. (The owner still has Refresh the app in Settings.)

## 5.4 · Nov 21, 2026
NuRenaissance account.
- The bottom of Home has a **NuRenaissance account** link: sign in once at nrnszn.com (Google or an e-mail link) and come straight back here signed in. The same account works on Certified, NuDirectory and luoh. Once signed in, the link shows your name. Nothing on Soul Vault needs an account; it only appears once accounts are switched on.

## 5.3 · Nov 19, 2026
An Admin link at the bottom of Home.
- The row of small links at the bottom of Home (Privacy, Add to Home Screen, Offline music, Refresh) now ends with **Admin**. It opens the owner sign-in; if you are already signed in on this device it takes you straight in. Visitors only ever see the sign-in.

## 5.2 · Nov 18, 2026
The version and What's new are for the owner only.
- The bottom of Home no longer shows "Version 5.2 · What's new" to visitors. You still see it when you are signed in as the owner.
- Settings has a **What's new** row (next to Refresh the app) that opens the history.
- The What's new page is still at /whats-new, but nothing public links to it and it is kept out of search results.

## 5.1 · Nov 18, 2026
Phone notifications and simpler version numbers.
- **Phone notifications:** turn them on from the Home footer ("Notifications") to hear about new music and new shows.
- **Automatic notifications** (Nick): Settings > Fans & activity > Notify fans has two switches, New music and New shows. Off until you turn them on.
- **Update pop-up:** when the site updates while you have it open, a bar says "A new version is ready. Tap to update".
- **Support** on Home opens the payment page inside the app.
- **What's new** page (this one) and a short version number instead of a long code.

## 5.0 · Nov 16, 2026
Everything before the version list started.
- Soul Vault is soulnicolas.com: Home, Music (the Vault), Tour, Press, Media and Lore.
- Studio for releases, songs, covers, lyrics, voice notes and YouTube/SoundCloud songs; Settings, Shop, Milestones and the Soul Tribe fan list.
- Everything runs on Cloudflare: songs, pictures, shares, plays, sign-ups and the "Inside the Tribe" newsletter (first send Fri Nov 20).
- Installable, works offline, offline music, smart links, synced lyrics and Suggest timings.
