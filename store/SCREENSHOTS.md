# Screenshots for the stores

Real screenshots from the real app look best and are quickest to take on your Mac: **Xcode > Simulator** (iPhone 16 Pro Max for Apple's biggest size) or your own phone. Use the live site so the music and covers are real. Take them with the songs already loaded.

## Sizes (as I know them; confirm in each console)
- **Apple:** iPhone 6.9-inch (1320×2868) is required. Make the app iPhone-only to skip iPad screenshots. 3 to 10 images.
- **Google Play:** at least 2 phone screenshots (320 to 3840 px, any side no more than twice the other). Also a **feature graphic, 1024×500**, and a 512×512 icon (`public/icon-512.png`).
