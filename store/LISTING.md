## Long description
Soul Vault is the home of Nicólas Soul, a heal hop artist from Kenya based in Nashville.

- **Music:** every release in one place, with lyrics that follow along, credits, and songs you can save to listen offline.
- **Videos and photos:** music videos, freestyles and press photos.
- **Lore:** the story behind the music, a timeline of releases, shows and milestones.
- **Tour:** the next show, the Road to 100 Shows, and how to book.
- **Soul Tribe:** join the fan list for new music and show news.

Works offline: the app and the songs you saved open with no connection.
