# Real-phone checklist: Soul Vault

A computer test cannot prove these. Do them on a real iPhone (and an Android if you have one). Write the date and what you saw. Anything that fails goes back to Claude with a screenshot.

- [ ] **Install:** Home footer > Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen with the right icon. Open it in airplane mode: saved pages still open.
- [ ] **Songs:** play, pause, skip, lock the phone: music keeps going and the lock-screen cover and buttons work.
- [ ] **Other apps:** open Soul Vault while Spotify or Apple Music plays. Their sound must **not** stop until you start a song.
- [ ] **Transitions:** More > Song transitions > Gapless. Two songs in a row: no long gap. (Crossfade and volume slider are not offered on iPhone by design.)
- [ ] **YouTube / SoundCloud songs:** start one, close Now Playing, lock the phone. Does it keep playing? (Unknown on iPhone.)
- [ ] **Song <-> Video:** Now Playing: switch Song to Video and back; the place in the song is kept.
- [ ] **Offline music:** Save offline on a release, airplane mode, play it.
- [ ] **Notifications** (once the keys are set): Home footer > Notifications > Turn on; Settings > Notify fans > send a test. It arrives with the app closed.
- [ ] **Share:** the Share button on a release opens the phone share sheet.
- [ ] **Update pop-up:** after a new version is published, "A new version is ready" shows; tap it.
- [ ] **Admin:** `soulnicolas.com/admin` signs in; Studio saves an edit; Settings and Studio fit the screen with no sideways scrolling.
- [ ] **Car:** if you use CarPlay or Bluetooth, check the cover and titles show (never tested).
