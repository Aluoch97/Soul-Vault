Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026 · Operator: Nicólas Soul (sole proprietorship), Nashville, Tennessee · Website: soulnicolas.com

Short version: I only collect what the site needs to work, to see which songs people love, and to email you if you ask me to. I don't sell your information, and there are no ads or tracking pixels here.

1. Overview and hosting

This website is a music app and fan site. It is hosted on Cloudflare. The music, release information, comments and sign-up lists are stored with Google (Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Apps Script), which also streams the audio you play. These providers handle basic technical data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the site and keep it secure.

3. Cookies and storage on your device

This site doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies of its own. (The Bandsintown shows list is a third-party service and may set its own; see section 4.) It saves a few small items in your browser’s own storage so it works well: your random listening ID, the name you used on a comment, your volume, a saved copy of the song list so it opens faster, your recent plays and play queue, and whether you dismissed the “Add to Home Screen” tip. You can clear these any time in your browser settings; the site will still work, but may forget your name and volume.

4. Other companies involved

  • Cloudflare: hosts the website.
  • Bandsintown: provides the Shows list on the Tour tab. Its widget loads from Bandsintown’s servers, which see your visit, and it is covered by Bandsintown’s own privacy policy.
  • Google: stores the music and data (Drive, Sheets, Apps Script), streams audio, sends the newsletter email, and serves YouTube thumbnails and subscriber counts.
  • YouTube: videos on the Home page show a picture first, loaded from YouTube, and only start YouTube’s player when you tap play. At that point YouTube (Google) sees your visit and may set its own cookies. I use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). The Press page works the same way: videos show a picture first and play inside the page only when you tap them.
  • Fonts: all fonts are stored on this site, so no font company sees your visit.
  • Links out: press articles, YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music, Bandcamp, SoundCloud, Instagram, TikTok, Threads and Facebook have their own privacy practices. I’m not responsible for them once you leave this site.

5. How your information is used

  • To run the site and play music.
  • To respond to messages and comments.
  • To send the newsletter to people who asked for it (you can opt out in every email).
  • To see which songs and releases are resonating, using anonymous listening counts.
  • To keep the site secure and fix bugs.

6. Sharing

I don’t sell your personal information, and I don’t “share” it for advertising. I share information only with the service providers above so they can run the site, or when required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, property, or safety.

7. How long it is kept

Listening records, comments, the fan list and the error log are kept until they are no longer useful or you ask me to remove your information. The error log is trimmed automatically to a few thousand entries. If you unsubscribe from the newsletter, I stop emailing you but may keep the record that you did, so I don’t email you again by mistake.

8. Where information is processed

Cloudflare and Google run servers in the United States and other countries. By using the site you understand your information may be processed in those places.

9. Children

The site is not intended for anyone under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The birth year on the sign-up form is optional. If you believe a child has given me information, email me and I will delete it.

10. Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter using the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete what I hold about you (your sign-up details, comments or fan-link name) by emailing me.
  • Skip the optional fields on the sign-up form. Only first name, email and consent are required.
  • Clear your browser storage to reset your listening ID and saved name.

Because listening records use a random ID rather than your name, I can’t look up “your” listening unless you tell me the ID stored on your device.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, I will update it here and change the effective date. If a change affects how I use your email, I will tell newsletter subscribers.
